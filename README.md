🎀 Hello Kitty Cozy Café & Room Simulator

A cozy, Pinterest-inspired kawaii café and room simulator built in a single self-contained HTML file. Pet and dress up an animated Hello Kitty, serve treats to visiting animal friends, bake fresh pastries in a 3-step mini-game, decorate your room, and snap downloadable Polaroid photos—with zero build tools or external asset dependencies.

📸 Preview

Tip: Snap a Polaroid in-game or take a screenshot of your favorite room theme, save it as preview.png in your repo, and uncomment the line below!

✨ Features

💕 Interactive Hello Kitty

Custom Animated SVG: Features Kitty's iconic silhouette, glossy bow, breathing bounce, natural blinking, ear wiggles, and happy curved eyes.

Tap or Rub for Headpats: Click or rub/drag across Kitty's head to earn Coins, Happiness, and Friendship XP with floating heart particles.

Cozy Nap Mode: Tuck Kitty in for a nap with sleepy animations and floating Zzz bubbles to restore Energy.

🍰 Drag-and-Drop Café & Bakery

Visiting Animal Friends: Serve Mochi Bunny, Honey Bear, Cream Puppy, and Matcha Frog by dragging their requested treats from the Bakery Counter directly onto them (or tap-to-serve on mobile!).

Feed Kitty: Drag any pastry onto Kitty to fill her Tummy meter and boost Happiness.

🥣 3-Step "Bake a Treat" Mini-Game: Restock your bakery counter and earn bonus coins by mixing batter, swirling pastel frosting, and adding strawberry sparkles.

👗 Live Dress-Up Boutique

Spend earned Coins to unlock and equip items in real time:

Signature Bows: Classic Cherry, Strawberry Pink, Lavender Polka, Golden Tiara, Sunny Daisy.

Cute Outfits: Blue Overalls, Pink Café Apron, Strawberry Lace, Star Pajamas, Mint Sailor.

Accessories: Heart Glasses, Baker Hat, Mini Teacup.

🏠 Cozy Room Themes & Interactive Secrets

4 Pastel Themes: Switch between Strawberry Milk, Matcha Mint, Lavender Dream, and Sakura Tea House—complete with scalloped café awnings and glowing fairy lights.

Arched Sky Window: Click the window to cycle between Sunny Morning, Pastel Sunset, and Starry Night with dynamic room lighting.

Windowsill Strawberry Plant: Water the sprout until it blooms and grows ripe strawberries, then harvest them for bonus Coins.

🎵 Synthesized Music Box: Click the music box on the wall shelf to play a gentle Web Audio API lullaby.

📸 Kawaii Polaroid Photo Mode

Capture your currently dressed-up Kitty inside a decorated Polaroid frame, type a custom caption, and download it as a PNG image.

💾 Auto-Save & Progression

Tracks Friendship Level, Coins, Care Meters (Happy, Tummy, Energy), Bakery Stock, and Café Stats automatically using localStorage.

🚀 Quick Start

Because the entire simulator is packaged into a single HTML file, there is no installation or build step required.

Clone the repository

git clone https://github.com/your-username/hello-kitty-cozy-cafe.git
cd hello-kitty-cozy-cafe


Play immediately
Double-click kitty_cozy_cafe.html to open it in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox).

🌐 Deploy Free on GitHub Pages

Rename kitty_cozy_cafe.html to index.html (or link to it directly).

Push your files to a GitHub repository.

Go to Settings → Pages, select your main branch as the source, and click Save.

Your playable cozy café will be live in seconds!

🛠️ Tech Stack

Structure & Styling: HTML5, CSS3 custom animations, Tailwind CSS (via CDN), Google Fonts (Fredoka & Nunito)

Graphics: Hand-crafted inline SVGs & HTML5 Canvas (for Polaroid PNG export)

Audio: Pure Web Audio API synthesizer (chimes, pops, water droplets, fanfares, and music-box lullaby—no external audio files needed)

State & Storage: Vanilla JavaScript + Browser localStorage
